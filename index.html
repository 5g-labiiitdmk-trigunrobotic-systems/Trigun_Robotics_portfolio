About Trigun Robotic Systems

We are pioneers in autonomous robotic solutions, combining AI, IoT, and advanced engineering to create transformative technologies.

Innovating for a Smarter Future

Trigun Robotic Systems Pvt. Ltd., incubated at IIITDM Kurnool, Andhra Pradesh, is an innovation-driven startup specializing in autonomous robotics and AI-powered drone systems. Incorporated in December 2024, the company integrates academic expertise with industrial research to deliver advanced solutions in aerial, underwater, and indoor robotic systems.

With a mission to address critical challenges in healthcare, defense, agriculture, and smart city infrastructure, Trigun Robotic Systems is developing scalable, high-precision robotic platforms designed for real-world impact. The company's product portfolio includes:

Through strong collaborations with IIITDM Kurnool and TiHAN at IIT Hyderabad, Trigun Robotic Systems combines cutting-edge AI, IoT, and advanced robotics technologies to deliver versatile and modular solutions for industry, research, and government applications. With an engineering-driven team and robust academic partnerships, the company emphasizes rigorous testing, safety protocols, and field validation, positioning itself as a key contributor to India's rapidly evolving robotics and autonomous systems ecosystem.
